Subject: Permission request — commercial use of the IIP RIASEC Markers (Russian/Kazakh adaptation)

Dear Professor Rounds and Professor Armstrong,

I am writing on behalf of BHS (Beta High School), Kazakhstan. We run career guidance sessions for students in grades 8–11. For our new version we would like to use 30 items from the IIP RIASEC Markers, Activities Set A: five items per RIASEC type, selected by loadings and content diversity (Armstrong, Allison & Rounds, 2008, JVB 73(2)). The items would be translated into Russian and Kazakh.

Our current pilot is free for participants, and we understand that this is permitted under your non-commercial terms. We would like to ask for written permission to continue using the adapted items in a paid service after the pilot. The items would be:

- cited in every report: Armstrong, Allison & Rounds (2008);
- used only in our own desktop application, not published as a standalone test;
- validated on our own sample: internal consistency and structure in Russian and Kazakh. We would be glad to share the aggregated results with you.

If you have conditions (a fee, attribution wording, reporting), please let us know.

Thank you for making these scales available to the field.
